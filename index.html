Choose how many benches are available in each detail. Add all shooters first, then the app randomly assigns unique bench numbers. Detail 1 is filled first, then Detail 2, Detail 3 and so on.

Running the draw reassigns all shooters in the current match. Each detail gets benches 1 to the number you choose, with no duplicate bench number inside a detail. If the last detail is not full, it simply contains the remaining shooters.

Benchrest for Score

Choose how many score cards are being shot today. Only that many Target cards are shown and counted. Rimfire and Centrefire are both available, with Custom, Heavy, Light, Sporter, Juniors and Ladies categories.

Benchrest Group

Choose the shooter Category and how many Group cards are being shot today. Only that many groups are shown and counted. The Results page calculates the Total Aggregate using only the number of Group cards selected for today.

International Rimfire Benchrest (IRB)

Choose the shooter Category first. 50m • 25 record rondels per card • 20 minutes per card. Best-edge scoring applies. X counts 10 points plus one X. Choose how many cards are being shot today and the totals/results adjust automatically.

Results

Group Total Aggregate uses all Group cards selected for today. If you choose 3 cards, it uses Group 1, Group 2 and Group 3 only. The app calculates the Total Aggregate automatically. Lowest Total Aggregate wins.

Finish Match

When the match is finished, this clears all shooters, details, bench numbers and scores so the next match starts empty.

Shoot Settings

Shooters belong to the current match only. When you press Clear Match & Start New, all shooters, details, bench numbers and results are removed.

Backup

Keep a copy of the shoot data or move it to another device.
